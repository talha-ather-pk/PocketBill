## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```
PocketBill is a simple invoice maker for freelancers. Create a professional PDF invoice on your phone, email it to your client, and track who has paid, without an account and without internet.

FAST
• Pick a client and their currency and payment terms fill in for you
• Saved items: type two letters and your usual services appear with their price
• Duplicate last month's invoice in one tap
• Totals, tax and discounts update as you type

PROFESSIONAL PDFs
• Clean A4 or US Letter invoices with your logo
• Long invoices run across pages with the table header repeated
• Accented names and currency symbols print correctly: José, Müller, Øresund, €, £, ¥, ₹, ₦, ₱
• Paid invoices get a PAID stamp with the date

SEND IN ONE TAP
• "Email to client" opens your email app with the address, subject, message and PDF ready
• Or send it with WhatsApp or any other app
• Reminders and receipts use the right wording automatically

KNOW WHO OWES YOU
• Home shows what you're owed in each currency and what's overdue
• Mark invoices paid or unpaid, with Undo
• Search by client, number or item

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no sign-up
• Works fully offline: PocketBill has no internet access at all
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Back up everything to one file you can keep anywhere, and restore it on any phone

FREE
Every feature is free, with no limit on invoices or clients.

PocketBill helps you create and track invoices. It does not give tax or legal advice; check your country's invoicing rules.
```
