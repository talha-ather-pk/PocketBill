PocketBill Privacy Policy

Effective: [RELEASE DATE]

PocketBill is an invoicing app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information. In short: your data stays on your phone. PocketBill has no account, no servers, and no internet access.

What PocketBill stores

The details you type in, such as your business profile, clients, invoices, saved items and your logo, are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The developer never receives them.

What PocketBill collects

Nothing. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools in the app.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to:

  • Sending an invoice: when you tap Email to client or Send another way, the PDF and message go to the app you pick (for example Gmail or WhatsApp). That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Saving a PDF or a backup: the file is saved where you choose, for example your phone's Downloads folder or Google Drive.
  • Moving to a new phone: if you set up a new Android phone by copying from the old one, Android may copy PocketBill's data to the new phone. PocketBill's data is excluded from Android's cloud backup.
  • Feedback: if you tap Send feedback, your email app opens with the app version and Android version filled in. Nothing from your invoices is attached.

Permissions

PocketBill asks for no permissions. It uses Android's own photo picker (for your logo), file picker (for backups) and share menu, which only give the app the single item you choose.

Children

PocketBill is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling PocketBill deletes all of its data from your phone. Backup files and PDFs you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete them where you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
